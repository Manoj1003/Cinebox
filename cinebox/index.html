      Convert videos

      Works with .mov, .mp4, .mkv, .avi and more. Conversion happens on this device, so long videos take time and need free memory. MP4, MOV and MKV at original size are copied without re-encoding, which takes seconds; choosing a smaller height or another format re-encodes and is slower. The converter downloads once (about 25 MB) the first time you use it.
